# Concepts and variants

A **concept** is a video idea with a hook of its own. A **variant** is one way of executing that same
concept. You deliver 3-4 concepts, each with 2 variants. The user picks.

## Where they come from

From the catalog, not from your head. Read the merged catalog and look for:

- The `hook: 5` items: which video starts with this?
- Repetitions: four different scenes of people serving food, three transfers in different vehicles, the
  same gesture five times. A repetition is a concept already made.
- Contrasts: what you expected versus what happened, expensive versus cheap, crowd versus empty.
- A chain: A leads to B leads to C (a route, a day, a process).
- What the agents already told you in their "three things that struck me most".

## Every concept needs, in writing, before building

```
short-name-with-hyphens
Hook (first second): what you see and what you read.
Promise: what you're telling the viewer is going to happen.
Structure: a list of blocks with approximate seconds and a mini hook every 3-5 s.
Target duration and BPM/grid.
Subject quota: how many cuts out of how many.
Tone and text: output language, style, how many captions.
Close: the last shot and what it reads.
Variant A: ...
Variant B: ...
```

If you can't write the hook in one sentence, the concept isn't ready.

## The 3-4 concepts have to differ from each other

Four photo dumps with different songs is not a delivery. Mix families:

| Family | What it is | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| Beat photo dump | many short shots on a beat grid | 10-20 s |
| Cinematic POV | few long shots, slow motion, serif text, almost no cuts | 15-25 s |
| Narrated documentary | voice-over with a story structure, music bed | 25-45 s |
| List / "things nobody tells you" | visible counter, one block per point | 25-40 s |
| Expectation versus reality | pairs of shots, hard cut in the middle | 15-25 s |
| Chain or route | each block enters through a vehicle, a place, a step | 30-45 s |
| Natural sound | no music and no voice, pure diegetic audio | 20-40 s |
| Character | freeze frame with a cutout and data cards | 15-30 s |

## The two variants of a concept

They have to read differently in the first 3 s. Pairs that worked:

- A silent with diegetic sound / B narrated
- A full at 35 s / B at 15 s with only the hook and the payoff
- A with the subject as protagonist / B pure b-roll
- A cut to the beat / B with long shots

What **doesn't** change between variants: the concept, the hook, the promise and the output language.
If those change, they're two concepts and you have to say so.

## Editing rules

- **The first shot is the video's decision.** It's the one with the highest `hook`, not the first
  chronologically.
- A mini hook every 3-5 s.
- Two consecutive cuts from the same scene, the same colour or the same framing read as a mistake.
  Change the scene, the scale or the direction.
- Don't open two blocks with the same composition.
- The close is planned: the last shot and the last caption, not whatever was left over.
- Captions carrying a fact (date, place, price) get verified; the ones carrying tone get written short.
- A corner stamp or tag comes in **with the cut**, never mid-shot, and lasts at least ~0.8 s or it can't
  be read.
- All on-screen copy is written in the resolved output language, and it is written *for* that language:
  a hook translated word for word from another language reads as translated. Rewrite it so it lands.

## Before sending to build

- Do the 3-4 concepts use different material? If two concepts are fighting over the same 6 good shots,
  split them: each one keeps a hook of its own.
- Is the subject quota met in each one separately? An average that works out is not enough.
- Does `common/` already have the crops, the music bed and the exported photos? If not, the builders
  will each do it their own way.
